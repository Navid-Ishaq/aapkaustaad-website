Sab se pehle: Yeh exam hai kis liye?

Certificate Municipal de Aptitud de Conductor de Taxi · Málaga, Andalucía

🚕 Certificate ka maqsad

Málaga municipality mein taxi driver ke taur par kaam karne ki aptitude ka certificate hasil karne ke liye municipal exam pass karna aur baqi prescribed requirements poori karna zaroori hai.

Yeh driver certificate hai; taxi ki business/vehicle licence alag legal cheez hai.

✅ Golden rule

4 subjects hain magar 4 separately pass karne walay papers nahi. Published instructions mein overall 30 points passing threshold di gayi hai; har subject ki alag pass threshold mention nahi.

Combined exam · Overall score
Source clarity: 2026 ki uploaded municipal convocatoria se dates, fee, requirements aur 4 topics liye gaye hain. 60 MCQs, four options, +1/−0.10/0 aur 30 passing points published 2026 exam-day instructions se liye gaye hain. Chaar Subjects — Kya Kya Parhna Hai?

01 · Callejero

Málaga municipality ke important roads, public offices, official centres, medical facilities, hotels, parks, tourist/cultural sites aur mukhtalif destinations tak direct routes.

Map + locations + routes

Ordenanza Municipal del Servicio de Taxi aur Reglamento del Taxi en Andalucía. Qawaneen, driver aur passenger ke rights aur duties, service regulations.

Taxi laws + ordinance
💶

Urban aur interurban taxi tariffs. Fare applicability, official fare tables aur charging rules. Old amount ko latest tariff se verify zaroor karein.

City + intercity fares
♿

04 · Accesibilidad

Disabilities aur limited mobility wale passengers ki respectful, safe aur accessible assistance. Official accessibility learning module parhhein.

Inclusive passenger service

11

Frequently Asked Questions

Jo sawal aksar candidates ko pareshan karte hain.

Kya har subject alag pass karna lazmi hai?
Published 2026 rules mein per-subject passing marks define nahi. Ek combined 60-question test ka overall minimum 30 points diya hai.
Exam mein kitne parts aur kitne sawal hain?
Syllabus ke 4 areas hain: Callejero, Normativa, Tarifas aur Accesibilidad. Total 60 MCQs hain. Har area ka exact questions quota official instructions mein specified nahi.
Kya wrong answer se marks kat-te hain?
Ji, har wrong answer par 0.10 marks deduct hote hain. Correct par +1 aur blank par 0.
Kitne marks par pass hota hoon?
At least 30 net points required according to available 2026 exam instructions. Upar calculator mein exact practice total check karein.
Fail ho jaaun to kya dobara fee deni hogi?
Ji. 2026 notice ke mutabiq har attempt ke liye nayi application aur €20 payment receipt deni hoti hai.
Exam ki jagah aur waqt kahan pata chalega?
Admission list ke saath Tablón de Edictos par. Pichhli sitting ki jagah ko next sitting ka venue assume na karein.
Exam pass karne ke baad seedha taxi chala sakta hoon?
Certificate ke liye remaining documents aur municipal process complete karein. Driver certificate, vehicle/taxi business licence aur employment permissions ek cheez nahi.
Kya age ya driving experience ki koi fixed requirement hai?
Aap ki 2026 convocatoria age aur minimum B-licence experience ka clear numerical rule nahi deti. Isay automatically “koi condition nahi” na samjhein; office se current ordinance ke mutabiq confirm karein.
